02 / Test protocol

Four passages,
four kinds of pressure.

The same source text is used for every voice, keeping family comparisons focused on the model rather than the prompt.

01

Programme intro

Short Cantonese presentation with English title and product terminology.

大家好，歡迎收聽我地嘅新節目《AI Unbox》。今日我地黎傾下微軟最新嘅宣佈：Trustworthy AI。
02

Pronunciation stress

Long, dense Cantonese with repeated near-homophones and uncommon combinations.

係呢條黃巷既巷口度有一隻狗，佢個名叫做黃汗寒狼。大家或者會覺得好奇怪，點解呢一隻狗會有一個咁得意既名呢？講起上黎，其實係有段古既。話說係東方有一個叫做黃汗寒狼牛牙雅夏蝦既國家，呢個國家既人都好鍾意養狗既，佢地養大既狗仲好靚同埋好強壯，但係佢地飼養狗既飼料同一般既都好唔同．原來佢地係用黃汗寒狼焊汗糠黎到養既，養大既狗仲吸引左各國各地既人黎購買添！其中以壓乞黑國、很肯國、特突國、能單騰國、華滑挖國、心足縮學咢確國、等登燈等國既人最多。而頭先我地所講既黃汗寒狼，呢種咁昂貴既狗，就係呢個國家所出產架啦。

Source: Cantonese Museum — original article

03

Office code-switching

Compact workplace dialogue with English verbs, nouns, and compound phrases.

我啱啱check咗今日個schedule，朝早十點有個project meeting。你記得meeting之前send份final version畀我，因為個client下晝就要confirm所有details。
04

Long-form mixed narration

Sustained English–Cantonese switching to expose pacing and continuity issues.

今日返到office之後，我第一時間開email check下有冇urgent message。跟住project manager話個client想改部分requirements，所以我哋臨時安排咗一個meeting。大家討論咗差唔多一個鐘，最後決定先做一個prototype，下星期再demo畀client睇。雖然個schedule有啲tight，但係成個team都覺得呢個approach比較practical。

03 / General insight

What to listen for.

These are evaluation lenses, not final rankings. Listening notes should decide the shortlist.

680

playable baseline samples

Neutral family-wide testing makes model character audible before styles, prosody, or generative controls are introduced.

01

Native accent before fidelity

A polished voice is not automatically convincing Cantonese. Prioritise tones, particles, rhythm, and Hong Kong code-switching.

02

English transitions matter

Cases 03 and 04 reveal whether English insertions remain fluid or interrupt Cantonese cadence.

03

Long-form stability separates models

Listen for persona drift, rushed clauses, unexpected pauses, and declining clarity near the end of case 04.

04

Tune only after shortlisting

Compare baseline quality first. Apply temperature, top-p, styles, or prosody only to the strongest candidates.
